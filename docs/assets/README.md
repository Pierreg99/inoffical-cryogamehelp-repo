# Image and font credits

All `games/*.webp` images are genuine game artwork or publisher screenshots. Sources, retrieval dates, and rights notes are listed in [`sources.json`](sources.json) and available through the website's image credits dialog.

- Dragon Ball Legends: official Bandai Namco Entertainment game website.
- Pokémon TCG Pocket: official game website.
- Pokémon species artwork: official Pokémon website Pokédex assets.
- Pokémon TCG Live and Pokémon GO screenshots: official publisher App Store listings.

These third-party assets remain with their respective rights holders and are **not** licensed under this repository's MIT license. WebP conversion and resizing do not change their ownership.

DM Sans and Space Grotesk are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License. Their notices are included in `fonts/`.

`favicon.svg` is an original project graphic. Existing original SVG documentation diagrams retain the repository license.
